# Tether 申诉信模板（稳妥化措辞版）

> ⚠️ 使用边界：
> 本模板供取证分析辅助使用，不构成法律意见。涉及资金定性与法律表述时：
> - 用「数据显示 / 初步分析表明 / 据链上记录」替代「证明 / 确认 / 肯定」
> - 不承诺结果，不承诺解冻
> - 重大案件建议由客户咨询持牌律师后再发出

## 联系渠道

- 官方邮箱：compliance@tether.to（首选）/ legal@tether.to / support@tether.to
- 主题格式：`USDT Freeze Appeal - Address <T开头地址>`
- 审核周期：2-8 周（官方口径，不做承诺）

## 邮件正文模板（英文）

```
Subject: USDT Freeze Appeal - Address <ADDRESS>

Dear Tether Compliance Team,

I am writing to request a review of the freeze applied to USDT held
in the address <ADDRESS> (TRON network).

1. Freeze details: According to public blockchain records, this
   address shows a BanList (BL) marker on <FREEZE_DATE> (UTC).
   The amount shown in the marker is <AMOUNT> USDT.

2. Source of funds: Based on the transaction records available to me,
   the USDT in this address was received as
   <business payments / loan repayments / salary> from
   <COUNTERPARTY DESCRIPTION> between <DATE_RANGE>.
   Please find attached: business contracts, invoices, chat records,
   and identity documents for reference.

3. Nature of transactions: These transfers were intended as bona fide
   commercial transactions. Based on my knowledge, I was not aware of,
   nor did I participate in, any prohibited activity by the
   counterparty. If there are concerns about the counterparty,
   I would welcome the opportunity to provide further clarification.

4. Current balance: At the time of the freeze, the remaining balance
   was <AMOUNT> USDT. This appears small relative to the transaction
   volume shown on-chain, which may suggest this is an
   identity-related flag rather than seizure of illicit funds.
   I respectfully leave this determination to your review.

5. Post-freeze conduct: Since the freeze, I have ceased all activity
   on this address.

I kindly request Tether to review the above information and, if
appropriate, separate any funds with legitimate provenance from any
contaminated funds and unfreeze <ADDRESS>.

Attachments (for reference):
- Identity / business registration documents
- Business contracts and invoices
- Wallet ownership proof (signed message)
- Transaction records

Sincerely,
<NAME>
```

## 附件清单（硬通货）

1. KYC：身份证/护照/营业执照
2. 资金来源：银行流水、交易所提现记录
3. 商业真实性：合同、发票、聊天记录、物流单据
4. 钱包所有权：签名消息（私钥签名证明控制权）
5. 时间线说明：资金进入时间 vs 地址被标记时间

## ⚠️ 红线（对外交付前逐条检查）

- 不使用「证明 / 确认 / 保证 / 肯定」等绝对化表述
- 不承诺解冻结果、不承诺时间
- 不代管客户资金、不代付任何费用
- 提醒客户：任何 TG/微信声称付费解冻的都是诈骗
- 大额/复杂案件建议客户咨询持牌律师；本模板非法律意见
